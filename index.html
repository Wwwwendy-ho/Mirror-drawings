Sinopia

Load a photo of your drawing and the reference you worked from, then compare them. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Pan: drag. Zoom: scroll wheel or pinch. Hide / show: double-tap or double-click an image (or H) to flick it away and back, like blinking between the two. Fit: Fit all or F centres both images and lines the drawing up with the reference (your dots first, otherwise an automatic match that only applies when it is confident; an alignment you adjusted by hand is kept). Fit or 0 fits one image; with Link on the other follows. Shift+F fits each image on its own and clears the alignment. Z undoes.
  • Link keeps pan and zoom in sync; turn it off to move one image alone. In Overlay, tap the Drawing or Reference name above the image to select it: that image moves when Link is off, it is the one double-tap hides, and the buttons beside the names (mirror, rotate, fit and so on) act on it.
  • Overlay compare modes: Tint (default) shows the reference in red and your drawing in blue; where they agree it goes dark, and a red or blue fringe is exactly where you are off. Blend fades one into the other. Wipe drags a curtain: your drawing on one side, the reference on the other. Outline lays the reference's contours as a red line drawing over your drawing. For a pure flicker, use Blend at 100% and double-tap the top image.
  • Load: drop a file onto a pane, paste with Ctrl/⌘+V, or use the pane's own buttons. Every new photo is added to that pane's shelf (up to 12 photos each) and put on the easel; nothing is overwritten.
  • Shelves: once a pane holds two or more photos, its header shows ‹ 3 / 5 ›. The arrows (or , older and . newer, for the selected image) step through the photos in the order they were added; the count opens a strip of thumbnails. Each photo keeps its own mirror, zoom, placement and dots. Remove takes away the photo on the easel, and asks first. If a shelf is full or the browser is short of space, you are asked before anything is deleted.
  • Known limit: placement is saved per photo, not per pair. If you switch the reference to a different photo, the drawing keeps its own placement and may need Line up again.
  • Line up: press Line up (or D) and follow the four steps: tap two clear, far-apart spots on the reference (a vermilion circle, then a blue square), then the same two spots on your drawing. The app moves, resizes and turns the drawing to match, and you can drag any dot to fine-tune. Use one spot matches position only. Auto tries without dots and only moves anything when it is sure; a pencil sketch against a photo is often too different, so dots are the dependable way.
  • Retake: adding a new photo to a Drawing pane that already has one keeps the alignment: the new photo is matched to the previous one and inherits its placement. The old photo stays on the shelf, one step back. Previous flips to the photo just before the one on the easel.
  • Camera: the app asks for the camera at most once per session. After you take a photo the camera stays on standby for 2 minutes (a dot on the Camera button shows it) so the next shot is instant, then it is turned off. It is also turned off when you leave the page. Whether your browser remembers your answer for next time is up to the browser; see the README.
  • Saved on this device: your photos (as stored JPEG/PNG/WebP copies, usually 0.4 to 1 MB each) and view settings are kept in this browser's local storage (IndexedDB) so they come back after a reload. Clear all saved photos removes every one of them.

Drawing Compare

Load a photo of your drawing and the reference you worked from, then compare them. Nothing leaves your browser.

  • Pan: drag. Zoom: scroll wheel or pinch. Fit: double-click a pane.
  • Link keeps pan and zoom in sync; turn it off to move one image alone (in Overlay, pick which with Move).
  • Load: drop a file anywhere on a pane, paste with Ctrl/⌘+V, or use File / Camera / Paste.
  • Saved on this device: your images and view settings are kept in this browser's local storage (IndexedDB) so they come back after a reload. They are never uploaded. Clear saved images removes them.
